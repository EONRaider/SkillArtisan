# Seed draft — narrative-arc-builder

Rough, blank-slate ask fed to every comparison arm's authoring workflow.

---

I keep handing people (and Claude) a rough bullet-point outline for a presentation and
getting back... the same bullet points, just reworded and put on slides. What I actually
need is someone to find the *story* in the material — what's the tension or question this
presentation is actually resolving, what's the through-line connecting the sections, and
is the pacing right (not five slides of setup before you get to the one thing everyone
actually cares about).

I want a skill that takes a rough outline and restructures it around a real narrative arc
— setup, tension/complication, resolution — while flagging the specific structural
problems it finds: a buried lede (the most important point sitting in slide 8 instead of
near the front), no clear throughline connecting sections, a data-heavy section dropped in
the middle of a story-heavy flow with no transition, or a "conclusion" that just
summarizes rather than resolving the tension the opening set up.

It shouldn't force a rigid three-act structure onto something that's already well-paced —
if the outline already has a clear arc, it should say so rather than reshuffling something
that doesn't need it.
